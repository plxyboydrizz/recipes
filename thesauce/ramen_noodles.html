Ingredients

this is what you will need typeeee

  • 2 packs of instant ramen noodles (any flavor you like)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup sliced green onions
  • 1/2 cup narutomaki
  • 1/2 cup sliced carrots
  • 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup sliced cooked chicken or tofu (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ginger powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

Instructions

follow these steps to make the best ramen noodles ever :p

  1. In a large pot, bring the water to a boil. Add the instant ramen noodles and cook according to the package instructions (usually 3-4 minutes). Drain the noodles and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet or wok, heat the vegetable oil over medium-high heat. Add the sliced green onions, narutomaki, carrots, and bell peppers. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until the vegetables are slightly tender.
  3. If using cooked chicken or tofu, add it to the skillet and stir-fry for an additional 2 minutes until heated through.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic powder, ginger powder, and red pepper flakes (if using).
  5. Add the cooked ramen noodles to the skillet with the vegetables and protein. Pour the sauce over the noodles and toss everything together until the noodles are well coated and heated through, about 2-3 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat and serve the ramen noodles hot. You can garnish with additional sliced green onions or a boiled egg if desired. Enjoy your delicious Walkemdown wit da Ramen Noodles!
