Ingredients

this is what you will need typeeee

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (optional)
  • 1/2 cup diced vegetables (optional)

Instructions

follow these steps to make the best omelette ever :p

  1. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and lightly grease it with butter or cooking spray.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet and let it cook undisturbed for about 2-3 minutes until the edges start to set.
  4. If using cheese and vegetables, sprinkle them evenly over one half of the omelette.
  5. Using a spatula, carefully fold the other half of the omelette over the filling. Cook for an additional 1-2 minutes until the cheese is melted and the omelette is cooked through.
  6. Remove from heat and slide the omelette onto a plate. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious La Omelette!
